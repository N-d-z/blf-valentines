Our heart

A seat at the table

Every heart deserves a place where it is seen, celebrated, and strengthened.

At Body Life of Families, love is never a spectator sport. We build safe, warm spaces where marriages flourish and singles thrive, where the wisdom of decades meets the hope of new beginnings, and nobody sits at the edge of the room.

This Valentine's, every part of our family gathers around one candlelit table. Not to split the room into “taken” and “waiting,” but to celebrate covenant, cultivate connection, and remind one another that real love is practised in real life.

  • Sistaz & Suitorz

    Our vibrant singles, celebrated for who they are today, not only for who they might meet.

  • Boaz Brigade

    Men of honour and intention, setting the standard for kindness and courage.

  • Mothers Collection

    Grace, wisdom and nurture from the women whose love has held families together.

  • Couples & engaged partners

    From newly promised to decades strong, rekindling romance and renewing covenant.

The evening, in six moments

Curated evening experiences

From the first glass at arrival to the last song of the night, each part of the programme is designed to make you laugh, reflect, and leave a little more in love.

First, on arrival

The Arrival Experience

Welcome

Signature craft mocktails, red-carpet photography, and icebreaker “Connection Cards” to warm up every table.

Then

The Couple Connection Challenge

On stage

A lighthearted stage game uncovering fun truths about how well you really know each other, with prizes for the winners.

By candlelight

Love That Lasts

Real stories

Fireside wisdom from couples married 10+ years on resilience, forgiveness, and choosing grace every day.

Over dinner

Love, Faith & Real Life Panel

Candid conversation

A moderated, honest conversation on intimacy, conflict, finances, and what godly romance looks like today.

A quiet pause

The Love Letter Moment

Reflection

A dedicated, unhurried moment to handwrite notes of appreciation to a spouse, a mentor, or a friend.

To close the night

The Celebration Finale

Finale

A live DJ set, community awards for Best Coordinated Look, Longest Married and Table Spirit, and the “Love in Action” commitment challenge.
