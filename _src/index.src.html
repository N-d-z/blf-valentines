The evening

How the night unfolds

Doors open at 4:00 PM. Dinner is served to every guest, and the evening moves through six moments, from the red carpet to the final song.

  1. {{i:camera}}ArriveFrom 4:00 PM
  2. {{i:puzzle}}PlayCouples on stage
  3. {{i:flame}}ListenReal stories
  4. {{i:mic-vocal}}TalkOpen panel
  5. {{i:feather}}WriteA quiet pause
  6. {{i:party-popper}}CelebrateAwards & DJ
1

Red carpet & welcome

The Arrival

Walk the red carpet, pose for your photo and take a signature mocktail. Connection Cards on every table get the conversation going before the first course.

2

Games on stage

The Couple Connection Challenge

Couples race through blindfolded tasks and quick-fire questions that reveal how well they really know each other. The room cheers and the winners take home prizes.

3

Real stories

Love That Lasts

Couples married ten years and more share what carried them through: the hard seasons, the forgiveness, and the small choices they make every day.

4

Honest conversation

Love, Faith & Real Life Panel

A moderated conversation on intimacy, conflict, money and faith, with questions from the floor. Married or single, you'll leave with something to talk about on the drive home.

My dearest,

thank you for every ordinary day…

{{i:heart}}
5

A quiet pause

The Love Letter Moment

The room goes quiet. You get a card, a pen and a few unhurried minutes to write to someone who matters: your spouse, a friend, a parent or a mentor.

6

Awards & live DJ

The Celebration Finale

The DJ takes over, the night's awards go out for Best Coordinated Look, Longest Married and Table Spirit, and we close together with the Love in Action challenge.
